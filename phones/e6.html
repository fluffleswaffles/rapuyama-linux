Nokia E6

In active development.

Nokia E6 is a small QWERTY smartphone, originally running Symbian^3. Also the first rapuyama-linux support target.

Default (and recommended) desktop is Yamashell.

Installation

See the installation page for full installation.

Steps below are for rapuyama-linux LITE installation.

Data won't persist between reboots.

Requirements

  • Phone must be hacked
  • Bluetooth/USB connection is needed for transfering files

Device-specific steps

  1. Download latest e6bfix.sis, Image and e6.dtb from releases page (make sure they're for E6)
  2. Transfer files to your phone
  3. Place Image and e6bfix.sis to the root of your E: drive
  4. Install e6bfix.sis to your C: drive
  5. Launch e6bfix.sis

Notes

Known issues

Sometimes e6bfix app gives error -71. This error means 'out of memory'. To fix this, reboot your phone, wait 1-1.5 minutes and try launching again. If that doesn't help, try plugging in the micro USB cable into your PC in Nokia Suite mode.

On some devices, keyboard and hardware keys may not work. On some, GPU may not start. If that happens, please open up a new issue on Github, following issue templates.
